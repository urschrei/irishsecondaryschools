The files cover the schools aided by the Department of Education in the 2025/26 school year. They are derived from the Department's school lists, with locations, enrolment history, area deprivation and distances between schools.

The roll number is the Department's identifier for a school. Use it to join the files to each other and to other Department datasets. The map can also download the schools that match a search or filters, as GeoJSON.

Columns and limitations

The README in the project repository describes each column and the limitations of the data. You should read the limitations before you compare enrolment across years or use the deprivation scores.

Licence and credit

The sources are published under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 licence. When you use the files, you should credit the Department of Education, Pobal and Tailte Éireann. The project's code and its own additions to the data are dedicated to the public domain under CC0.

The base map is from OpenFreeMap, with data from OpenStreetMap contributors.

Checksums

To check a download, compare its SHA-256 checksum with the value below. On macOS or Linux, run shasum -a 256 followed by the file name.

Errors in the data

To report an error, open an issue and give the roll number of the school. The Department of Education takes reports of errors in its own data at statistics@education.gov.ie.
